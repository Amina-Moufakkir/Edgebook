# Edgebook AI

An AI coaching platform that helps traders improve their decision-making through structured journaling, behavioral analysis, and evidence-based reflection.

Edgebook AI does not predict the market — it coaches the trader, measuring decision quality rather than performance alone.

> **Discover Your Edge.** _A mirror, not a map._
